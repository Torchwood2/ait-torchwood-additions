# Changelog

## 0.2 — The Wilds (Beta)

### New dimension: The Wilds
- Reached by TARDIS: it's in the dimension list on the console like any other
- Three biomes on Large Biomes-sized terrain: the **Wild Jungle** (with Torchwood trees), the vast **Great Desert** (sand and sandstone) and **The Mire**, a misty mangrove bog of mud and murky water
- The only place to find **Rift Ore**

### Torchwood
- A full wood set: log, wood, stripped log and wood, planks, leaves, sapling, stairs, slab, fence, fence gate, door, trapdoor, button and pressure plate
- Torchwood trees grow in the Wild Jungle; saplings grow anywhere

### Rift
- Rift Ore and Deepslate Rift Ore (diamond pickaxe or better), Rift Shards and Block of Rift
- Rift tools (sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe) and armour: diamond-level mining, a little less protection than diamond, easier to enchant

### Chairs
- A chair in every wood (oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, bamboo, crimson, warped, Torchwood); right-click to sit

### Guns
Built on AIT's gun system (loaded with AIT's Staser Bolt Magazine, aim with use):
- **Torchwood Service Pistol**: quick, accurate when aimed
- **Rift Carbine**: big magazine, very fast fire, sprays from the hip
- **Rift Lance**: slow, hard-hitting, pinpoint when aimed
- All repairable with Rift Shards

### Desktops and exteriors
- **Torchwood Hub** and **Wilds Lodge** desktops
- **Torchwood**, **Rift** and **Desert** police box looks

### Notes
- Textures for the new content are placeholders and will be replaced

## 0.1 — First release (Beta)

For Fabric 1.20.1 and Adventures in Time 1.2.12+.

### Exteriors
- New TARDIS shapes, each with its own interior door and opening doors:
  - **Police Box** with 10 paint jobs (Default, Alternate, Alternate II, Bad Wolf, Coral, Dino, Gamble Box, Purple, Rhamnous, Tokomak)
  - **Blueberry Box**, a double-doored police box sized to match AIT's
  - **K2 Phone Booth** with 7 finishes, and **K6 Phone Booth** with 5
  - **The Onion** in 9 colours: its door is a beam that appears when it opens, with a bloop, and it always travels with its own takeoff (10.5 s) and landing (12 s) animations
- 26 extra variants for AIT's own exteriors, including Harmony, Zen, Inverted and Toywardian police boxes, Memory Box (and Classic), Atrium Box, Mayhem Box, TARDIM, DO7, Glasgow and Burnt Capsule

### Desktops
- 21 new interiors: Blueberry, Classic Mystical, Classic Neon, Copper, Fugitive, Lady Liberty, McGann, Medieval, Missy, Mystical, Pride, Ravencrest, Rosewood, Second, Second (Alt), Skra TARDIS, Third, Third (Alt), Trenzalore Missy, Tribute and Wooden

### Sonics
- 12 new sonic casings: Hedgehog, Sonic Hammer, Copper Cane, Umbrella, War Master, Blunt, classic Pertwee, Baker, Davison, McGann and War Doctor sonics, and an Aperture portal gun

### Atrium
- Atrium ore in the Overworld (stone and deepslate), Atrium and Block of Atrium
- Atrium Fuel (5,000 artron), Energized Atrium (15,000) and Atrium Core (fills the tank): right-click a console to refuel

### Blocks
- 44 roundel styles, each as a full roundel, half roundel, stairs and slab
- Grate, Thick Grate, Rusty Grate and Rusty Thick Grate, each with a trapdoor
- Hartnell Wall

### Sounds and GUI
- Type 70 takeoff, landing and flight sounds
- Police box door sounds for the police box shapes
- Four built-in resource packs for AIT's screens: Blue Shift, Red Shift, Greyed and LeBron James
- AIT: Torchwood Additions logo on the title screen (with AIT's custom menu on)

### Compatibility
- **Gallifrey**: atrium is shared between the two mods (recipes, console refuelling and one-for-one swaps), only one lot of atrium ore generates, and all 25 of Gallifrey's sonic casings appear on AIT's sonic
- Requires Supplementaries and Amendments
